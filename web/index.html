Reading network…

Let an agent trade. Never let it take unrestricted control.

Holdfast is a non-custodial vault and trading agent on HSK Chain. An agent key can trade one pool inside limits the owner sets on chain, and it cannot withdraw. The contract enforces those limits whatever the agent or its model is told to do.

  1. You write a strategy in plain English. A model turns it into a policy that must pass a schema.
  2. Deterministic code trades it. No model runs in the trading path.
  3. The vault checks every trade against your limits and refuses anything outside them.

Stage: testnet. No mainnet deployment or swap has happened. The contract is not audited, no outside user has tried it, and profit is not claimed.

What the limits do

The contract enforces

  • One router and two tokens, fixed at deployment
  • A cap per trade, an hourly budget, a daily budget and a lifetime cap, per token
  • A minimum price on every sale
  • A price band around the pool's own time-weighted average, checked on the price the trade leaves behind
  • An agent key that expires within 30 days, and can be ended at once by the owner or a guardian
  • Short trade deadlines, and no replay of a trade
  • A pause and a halt, and no withdrawal by the agent. The owner can always withdraw
  • A stop anyone may fire once the average price crosses the owner's trigger, whose proceeds stay in the vault

It does not prevent

  • Bad trades inside the limits. A stolen key can sell at the minimum price until a limit stops it
  • A price held away from the market for a whole averaging window. That costs an attacker real money, measured below, but it is possible, so the floor stays the backstop
  • Loss from a falling price before the owner or the stop acts. The stop sells in tranches and each one can still lose
  • Gas spent by the agent key, which is separate from the token caps

Who can do what

The model is outside the trust boundary. It only ever produces a policy that is checked against a schema, and no model runs in the trading path. The vault does not trust the agent either.

Not trusted

Model

Turns a plain-English strategy into a typed policy. Prompt injection stops here: it can only yield a policy the schema accepts.

Market data

Pool reads and flashblock frames. A node can lie or lag, so the vault reads the price again itself.

Agent process

Decide

Deterministic code: a pure function of market and policy, and no model call.

Execute

Write-ahead ledger, one send at a time, an optional preflight by eth_call, and reorg handling. Holds one key, which cannot withdraw.

Enforced on chain

HoldfastVault

Checks caps, budgets, floor, band, deadline, replay and expiry. Refuses, whatever it is told.

Router and pool

Fixed at deployment. Tested on a fork of the real HSKSwap router, and on our own test market on testnet.

    Where it could go

    A proposal: nothing here is built, and there are no customers or revenue. Autonomous trading is the first use. The larger product would be the vault itself, as security infrastructure for other teams' trading agents.
