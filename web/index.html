Reading network…

Let an agent trade. Never let it take unrestricted control.

Holdfast is a non-custodial vault and trading agent on HSK Chain. An agent key can trade one pool inside limits the owner sets on chain, and it cannot withdraw. The contract enforces those limits whatever the agent or its model is told to do.

  1. You write a strategy in plain English. A model turns it into a policy that must pass a schema.
  2. Rules or a model propose a trade. Deterministic checks approve the size, price and risk before execution.
  3. The vault checks every trade against your limits and refuses anything outside them.

Mainnet v4 is deployed. Testnet v3 is available too. The recorded mainnet checks show an unfunded, unconfigured vault with zero trades. Deployment is complete; profitable trading is not demonstrated. Choose a network and inspect the proof.

How to use Holdfast

  1. Pick mainnet or testnet. Check the chain number and vault address. Changing this view only changes what the page reads.
  2. Inspect the current state. Open the vault and venue sections for balances, caps, guard status and read timestamps. A deployed vault may still be unable to trade.
  3. Try an attack safely. “Try to break it” asks the selected chain to simulate a call. No transaction is sent and no gas is spent.
  4. Follow a proof link. Every measurement names its environment and a source. Owner controls require your wallet, a matching chain and a transaction review.
What the evidence labels mean

Mainnet read: data read from the real network, without a trade. Testnet: free test tokens in our own market. Fork: a local copy of chain state. Local: tests or model evaluations. Replay: simulated decisions on recorded prices. A replay gain is not money earned.

Still open: mainnet funding and safety settings, explorer source verification, model-driven chain execution, operational monitoring and an external audit. No sustained profitable trading has been demonstrated.

What the limits do

The contract enforces

  • One router and two tokens, fixed at deployment
  • A cap per trade, an hourly budget, a daily budget and a lifetime cap, per token
  • A minimum price on every sale
  • A price band around the pool's own time-weighted average, checked on the price the trade leaves behind
  • An agent key that expires within 30 days, and can be ended at once by the owner or a guardian
  • Short trade deadlines, and no replay of a trade
  • A pause and a halt, and no withdrawal by the agent. The owner can always withdraw
  • A stop anyone may fire once the average price crosses the owner's trigger, whose proceeds stay in the vault

It does not prevent

  • Bad trades inside the limits. A stolen key can sell at the minimum price until a limit stops it
  • A price held away from the market for a whole averaging window. That costs an attacker real money, measured below, but it is possible, so the floor stays the backstop
  • Loss from a falling price before the owner or the stop acts. The stop sells in tranches and each one can still lose
  • Gas spent by the agent key, which is separate from the token caps

Who can do what

The model has no signing key. It can translate a strategy or, when explicitly enabled, propose buy, sell or hold. Deterministic checks and the vault constrain every proposal. The model engine is off by default and has only been evaluated locally and on recorded data.

Not trusted

Model

Turns plain English into a typed policy, or proposes a trade. Schema checks restrict its output; accepted output still needs risk checks.

Market data

Pool reads and flashblock frames. A node can lie or lag, so the vault reads the price again itself.

Agent process

Decide

Rules or an optional model proposal, checked against size, inventory, freshness and economics. A missing or stale proposal means hold.

Execute

Write-ahead ledger, one send at a time, an optional preflight by eth_call, and reorg handling. Holds one key, which cannot withdraw.

Enforced on chain

HoldfastVault

Checks caps, budgets, floor, band, deadline, replay and expiry. Refuses, whatever it is told.

Router and pool

Fixed at deployment. Tested on a fork of the real HSKSwap router, and on our own test market on testnet.
